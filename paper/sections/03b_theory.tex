\section{Theory and Validity Guarantees}

\paragraph{Intervention pair.}
For each item $i$, CertVIC records an original image, an edited image, and a
single declared intervention. The intervention pair is valid only when the edit
metadata, human review, and provenance certificate agree that the visual change
targets the intended factor.

\paragraph{Consistency indicator.}
Let $C_i \in \{0,1\}$ indicate whether the model answer changes when the task
requires a change, or stays fixed when the task requires no change. Let $a_i$
denote correctness on the original image.

\paragraph{Intervention-consistency gap.}
The primary estimand is $\Delta = \mathbb{E}[a_i] - \mathbb{E}[C_i]$ for the
pre-registered population. We analyze the bounded transform
$d_i=(a_i-C_i+1)/2 \in [0,1]$ for anytime-valid confidence sequences.

\paragraph{Optional stopping.}
Theorem statements in this paper are caveated: the confidence sequence is valid
under optional stopping for the declared bounded sequence and pre-registered
endpoint, not for post-hoc subgroup claims.

\paragraph{Descriptive intervals.}
Bootstrap intervals are descriptive only and are never certification.

\paragraph{Assumptions and limitations.}
The guarantee depends on the item-validity protocol, parser policy, and
pre-registered inclusion rules. It does not establish causal understanding or
deployment safety.
