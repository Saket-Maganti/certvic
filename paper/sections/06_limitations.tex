\section{Limitations}

Our claims are deliberately scoped. (1) \emph{Edit realism}: synthesized edits may
contain artifacts; we mitigate with edit-quality gates and human review, keep
crude and photorealistic engines separate, and treat single-factor validity as a
measured (not assumed) quantity, but residual confounds are possible. (2)
\emph{Label dependence}: task validity depends on correct object labels; unverified
labels are restricted to control edits. (3) \emph{Scope}: domains and edit
families are limited, and findings do not generalize beyond the tested
families/domains. (4) \emph{Models}: we evaluate open-local models on free compute;
any optional frontier reference is non-core, version-dated, and disabled by
default, so results may not transfer to closed models. (5) \emph{Statistics}:
certification is conditional on the stated certification policy and the
availability of an anytime-valid CS; descriptive summaries are not certification.
(6) \emph{Release}: recipe-first artifacts may require users to regenerate images
from source pointers. We make no deployment or safety claims and no claims about causal understanding.
