\paragraph{Model-dependent specificity limitation.}
The present evidence supports model-dependent specificity, not all-model specificity. In particular, Qwen shows a strong update-gap result but elevated sensitivity to irrelevant controls. Main conclusions must therefore be conditioned on this limitation: the control evidence is strongest for InternVL and LLaVA, while Qwen requires either a successful stricter Spurious V2 rerun or an explicit model-specific limitation.
